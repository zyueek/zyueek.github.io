Yueke Zhang (张跃柯)

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Vanderbilt University, advised by Prof. Yu Huang, with an expected graduation in 2027. Here is my CV.

My research develops trustworthy and efficient AI for software systems by connecting software engineering, security, and neuroscience. I study how human cognition can guide AI learning and adaptation, how program analysis and formal reasoning can improve reliability, and how contribution accountability can protect open-source ecosystems as AI agents become contributors. My long-term goal is to build agents that learn from experience, improve their capabilities, and carry out complex technical tasks while preserving user intent and human control.

My email is yueke.zhang@vanderbilt.edu. Feel free to contact me.
